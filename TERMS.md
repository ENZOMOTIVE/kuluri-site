# Kuluri – Terms of use

**Draft, 17 October 2026.** Written for the prototype built at the EU Agri-Hackathon 2026. A lawyer has not reviewed these terms. They must be reviewed for each country before Kuluri is used with real farms and workers.

## 1. What Kuluri is

Kuluri is a **prototype** for farms and seasonal workers. It helps them to:

- find each other;
- write down the terms they agree on;
- record the work done, and let the worker confirm or dispute those records;
- share totals with buyers and public administrations.

It runs on **synthetic data** provided by the hackathon organisers. Jobs, workers, chats and agreements in the demo are simulated.

## 2. What Kuluri is not

- Kuluri is **not a party** to any agreement between a farm and a worker.
- Kuluri is **not an employer**, a temporary-work agency, a recruitment agency or a labour intermediary. It does not hire, place, pay or supervise anyone.
- Kuluri does **not hold or transfer money**. Pay is agreed and paid directly between the farm and the worker.
- An agreement in Kuluri records the **agreed terms and the intention** to work together. It is **not an employment contract**. It does not replace:
  - the written contract or written statement of working conditions required by national law and by Directive (EU) 2019/1152;
  - any registration or declaration owed to the authorities.
- Kuluri does not give legal, tax or employment advice.

## 3. Disclaimer: if an agreement is not respected

The farm and the worker are each solely responsible for respecting what they agreed and what the law requires. **Kuluri does not guarantee that any party will respect an agreement**, including:

- that a worker will arrive, stay for the agreed period or perform the agreed tasks;
- that a farm will provide the agreed work, hours, pay, housing or working conditions;
- that a job, offer or availability posted in the app is accurate or still open.

Within the limits allowed by law, the authors of Kuluri accept **no liability** for any loss or damage caused by a party not respecting an agreement, or by relying on one. Examples include lost wages, lost harvest, travel or housing costs, and penalties or reductions of payments.

When an agreement is not respected:

1. Either party can record a **dispute** in the app. A dispute is kept as part of the record, and the other party cannot delete it.
2. Kuluri **does not decide** disputes and does not enforce agreements. Records, confirmations, disputes and fingerprints can be shown to a third party as evidence. Their legal value depends on that third party and on national law.
3. To resolve a dispute, contact the competent authorities or organisations, for example:
   - the national labour inspectorate;
   - a trade union or farmers' organisation;
   - a mediation service;
   - the courts.

Nothing in these terms limits liability that cannot be limited by law. Nothing in them limits the rights a worker or a farm has under employment or consumer law.

## 4. Records, transcription and translation

- Each person is responsible for checking what they record, confirm or send. **Do not confirm a record you have not checked.**
- Voice messages are transcribed and translated automatically by an AI service. Transcripts and translations **can be wrong**, especially for names, numbers, dates and amounts. The original message is always kept next to the translation. **The original wording is what counts.**
- Agreement terms suggested from a chat are a draft. Both parties must read and accept them.

## 5. Fingerprints and blockchain records

- When a record is confirmed, Kuluri computes a fingerprint, a salted SHA-256 hash. The fingerprint can be written to a blockchain.
- **No personal data goes on the blockchain.** That means no names, pay or identifiers, only the salted hash, a type and a time.
- A blockchain entry **cannot be changed or deleted**. It proves that a record existed in a given form at a given time. It does not prove that the content is true.
- If the salt is deleted, the on-chain entry can no longer be linked to the record.

## 6. Acceptable use

Users must not:

- post false jobs, false availability or false work records;
- **pressure a worker to confirm** a record or to withdraw a dispute;
- select or reject people on nationality, ethnic origin, sex, age, religion, disability or any other ground prohibited by law. Kuluri's matching uses skills, availability, languages and distance only;
- enter other people's personal data without a legal basis, or use the prototype with real personal data.

## 7. Personal data

The prototype is **not designed to process real personal data**. Before any pilot with real users, the following must be completed:

- a data protection impact assessment (GDPR, Regulation (EU) 2016/679);
- a privacy notice;
- a legal basis for each processing.
