Delete Your Account

You can request deletion of your Kuluri account and all associated data at any time.

  1. 1
    Send a deletion request by email

    Email us at privacy@kuluri.eu with the subject line "Account Deletion Request" and include the email address linked to your account.

  2. 2
    We confirm your identity

    We may send a short verification to the email on file to confirm the request is from you.

  3. 3
    Deletion completed within 30 days

    We will permanently delete your account and personal data within 30 days of your confirmed request.

Data that will be deleted

  • Your name and email address
  • Your profile, skills, and work history
  • Your wallet address and authentication data
  • Your messages and inbox
  • Your job applications and listings

Data we may retain

  • Signed job agreements — retained for the minimum period required under applicable EU labour law (typically up to 5 years), then permanently deleted.
  • Anonymised usage statistics that cannot be linked back to you.
  • Agreement hashes anchored on a public blockchain — cannot be removed due to the immutable nature of blockchain records, but contain no personal data.

To submit your deletion request:

privacy@kuluri.eu

We respond to all requests within 5 business days.
