# Deck

- **`kuluri_pitch.pdf`**: the final pitch deck for the EU Agri-Hackathon submission
  (Challenge 3). 12 slides: the problem in Kastelli Valley, the solution
  (connect, agree, hold), how it works, what changed for Maria and Nikos, the business
  model, the live demo and the team. Built in Canva by Juan Zotes Orcajo, with AI
  assistance for structure and wording.
- **`screenshots/`**: the app screens used on the site's step-by-step story.

The data behind the deck's claims, and the full AI-use declaration, are in
[`../DATA.md`](../DATA.md). The business-model figures are an illustrative
5-year model, not measured, and the slide says so.
