**Record once, share with control.**

Team 27 – Kuluri, EU Agri-Hackathon 2026, Challenge 3 "Flowing data, empowered collaboration".

## The problem

Buyers and public administrations increasingly ask farmers to prove fair working conditions. Farmers' labour records are scattered and hard to trust, so they repeat the same information for every buyer and authority.

## What Kuluri does

Farmer and worker agree a deal. The farmer records the work; the worker confirms it through their own link, in their own language. Buyers' requests and public-administration reports are filled in from that evidence, with totals only, never personal data. The farmer decides which buyer sees what.
