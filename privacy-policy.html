Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 October 2026  ·  Last updated: 18 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Kuluri ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, stores, and shares your personal data when you use the Kuluri mobile application and related services. By using Kuluri, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Who We Are

Kuluri is a platform that connects seasonal farm workers with farmers across Europe. The app is operated by Kuluri (eu.kuluri.app). For data protection inquiries, contact us at: privacy@kuluri.eu

2. Data We Collect

We collect the following categories of personal data:

  • Account data: Your name, email address, and profile photo (if provided via Google login).
  • Identity and wallet data: A cryptographic wallet address generated by Privy.io on your behalf. We do not store private keys.
  • Role and profile data: Whether you are a farmer or seasonal worker, your skills, work history, availability, and location preferences you choose to share.
  • Job and agreement data: Job posts you create or apply to, digital agreements you sign, work history records, and confirmed skills.
  • Communication data: Messages you send within the app's chat features.
  • Usage data: App activity, pages visited, and feature interactions, collected to improve the service.
  • Device data: Device type, operating system version, and app version for diagnostics.
  • Biometric data: If you enable biometric authentication, this is processed locally on your device by your operating system. We do not receive or store biometric data.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • Provide, operate, and improve the Kuluri platform.
  • Match workers with suitable farm job opportunities.
  • Enable digital signing of job agreements between farmers and workers.
  • Verify and display confirmed work history and skills.
  • Facilitate in-app messaging between farmers and workers.
  • Send notifications about job matches, agreement status, and platform updates.
  • Comply with applicable laws and prevent fraud or misuse.

The legal basis for processing under GDPR is: contract performance (Art. 6(1)(b)), legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)), and, where required, your explicit consent (Art. 6(1)(a)).

4. Data Sharing

  • Other users: Your public profile (name, skills, work history) is visible to farmers or workers you interact with. You control what you share.
  • Privy.io: Our authentication and embedded wallet provider. See Privy's Privacy Policy.
  • Supabase: Our database provider. Data is stored securely on EU infrastructure.
  • Blockchain: Signed agreement hashes may be anchored on a public blockchain. Personal details are not stored on-chain.
  • Legal authorities: We may disclose data if required by law.

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we remove your personal data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law (e.g. signed agreements may be retained for the duration required under applicable EU labour law).

6. Your Rights (GDPR)

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate data.
  • Delete your data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Restrict or object to processing.
  • Data portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@kuluri.eu. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures including encrypted connections (TLS/HTTPS), secure cloud infrastructure, and cryptographic digital signatures for agreements. Biometric authentication is processed entirely on-device.

8. Children's Privacy

Kuluri is not intended for users under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

9. International Transfers

Your data is stored and processed within the European Union. Where data is processed outside the EU, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place in accordance with GDPR Chapter V.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes via in-app notification or email. Continued use of Kuluri after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact Us
